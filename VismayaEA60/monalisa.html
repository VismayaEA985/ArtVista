Mona Lisa

One of the world's most famous masterpieces.

Mona Lisa

Artist Leonardo da Vinci
Year 1503–1506
Category Portrait Painting
Location Louvre Museum, Paris

Description

The Mona Lisa is one of the most celebrated paintings in the history of art. Created by the Italian Renaissance artist Leonardo da Vinci, the painting is admired for its mysterious smile, realistic details, and innovative artistic techniques. It attracts millions of visitors every year at the Louvre Museum in Paris.

Interesting Facts

😊 Famous Smile

The mysterious smile of Mona Lisa has fascinated art lovers for centuries.

🎨 Renaissance Masterpiece

It is considered one of the greatest achievements of the Italian Renaissance.

🏛 Louvre Museum

The painting is displayed behind bulletproof glass in the Louvre Museum.
