The Persistence of Memory

A Surrealist Masterpiece by Salvador Dalí

The Persistence of Memory

Artist Salvador Dalí
Year 1931
Category Surrealism
Location Museum of Modern Art (MoMA), New York

Description

The Persistence of Memory is one of Salvador Dalí's most famous paintings. It is known for its melting clocks, dream-like atmosphere, and surreal imagery. The artwork explores the concept of time, memory, and the subconscious mind, making it one of the defining paintings of the Surrealist movement.

Interesting Facts

⏰ Melting Clocks

The soft clocks symbolize the fluid and flexible nature of time rather than its strict measurement.

🎨 Surrealism

The painting is considered one of the greatest masterpieces of the Surrealist art movement.

🧠 Dream Inspired

Dalí believed dreams and imagination were important sources of artistic inspiration.
